# Getting started

The Knowledge Press app searches a library of 253 public-domain books on your
iPhone, iPad or Mac and writes an answer to your question from the passages it
finds. Everything it needs is inside the app: the books, the search index, and
the language model. It works in airplane mode.

## What you need

| Device | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone or iPad | iOS or iPadOS 26. Apple Intelligence must be on for on-device answers. |
| Mac | macOS 26 on Apple silicon. Apple Intelligence must be on for on-device answers. |
| Private Cloud answers | iOS, iPadOS or macOS 27, and a network connection. See [Answer engines](engines.md). |

Without Apple Intelligence, everything else still works: you can search, read
the passages, and browse and read the books. The answer area says why the
on-device engine is off.

## Ask your first question

1. Open the app. The Chat screen shows a line saying where answers come from
   and five suggested questions.
2. Tap a suggestion, or type your own in the box at the bottom, and tap
   **Send**.
3. The app searches the corpus, then writes an answer from what it found.
   The passages it used are listed under the answer.

For example, the suggestion "pillar of salt" searches the sacred texts for
that exact phrase. See [Asking a question](asking.md).

## Find your way around

The app has two parts: **Chat**, where you ask questions, and **Browse**,
where you read the books. Where they sit depends on the device.

=== "iPhone"

    Two tabs at the bottom: **Chat** and **Browse**.

    In Chat, the toolbar has buttons for **Chats** (your past conversations),
    **Settings**, **Delete**, and **Export as Markdown**.

=== "iPad"

    A sidebar on the left holds **New chat**, **Browse the corpus**, the
    answer engine and corpus scope, and your past chats. **Settings** is at
    the bottom of the sidebar and opens as a popover. The toolbar has **New
    chat**, **Delete** and **Export as Markdown**.

=== "Mac"

    The same sidebar as the iPad. **Settings** opens the standard Settings
    window (++cmd+comma++). The toolbar has **Export as Markdown** and
    **Delete**. The sidebar can be hidden with the toolbar's sidebar button.

## Ask from Siri and Shortcuts

The app adds one Shortcut, **Ask a Question**. Say "Ask The Knowledge Press"
to Siri, or add the shortcut to the Shortcuts app. It opens the app and asks
your question the same way the Chat screen does.

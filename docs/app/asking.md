# Asking a question

## Type a question

Type in the box at the bottom of the Chat screen and tap **Send**. Plain
questions work: "what does Marcus Aurelius say about anger?". So do exact
phrases: "pillar of salt". The app searches in two ways at once, by meaning
and by exact wording, so it finds a quoted phrase and a paraphrase of it.

While the app works, the box is disabled and **Send** becomes a **Stop**
button. Tap **Stop** to cancel the search or the answer.

## Choose where to search

The **Scope** picker narrows a question to part of the library. You find it in
the sidebar on iPad and Mac, and in **Settings**, under Corpus, on iPhone.

| Scope | Searches |
| --- | --- |
| all | Every book and every diary. |
| gutenberg | The books only. |
| diary | The diaries only. |
| A genre, such as philosophy or horror | That genre only. |

Narrowing the scope is the most useful change you can make. A small language
model can read only a few passages at once, so a search across every book
spreads them thin, and a search of the right genre spends them all where the
answer is.

The scope is remembered when you close the app. A question asked with a
scope other than **all** shows the scope as a small tag beside it.

## Start from a suggestion

An empty chat offers five questions. Each carries a tag naming the part of the
corpus it searches. Tapping one asks it at once, with that scope, without
changing your own scope setting.

| Tag | Question |
| --- | --- |
| sacred-texts | pillar of salt |
| world-literature | circles of Hell |
| diary | descriptions of the Great Fire of London |
| philosophy | the categorical imperative and moral duty |
| horror | a monster assembled from dead body parts |

## Ask again

**Ask again** runs the same question again with your current settings. It sits
under each question, and on a long press (or a right-click on a Mac) of the
question itself. Use it after you change the scope, the engine, or a search
or synthesis setting, to see what the change did. The answers from before stay
in the chat, so you can compare.

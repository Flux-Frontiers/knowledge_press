# Your chats

Each conversation is saved on the device when you ask your first question in
it. Nothing is sent to us.

## Find a past chat

=== "iPhone"

    Tap **Chats**, the sidebar button at the top left of the Chat screen. The
    list opens as a sheet. Tap a chat to open it. **New chat** is at the top.

=== "iPad and Mac"

    Past chats are in the sidebar, under the engine and scope controls. Use
    **New chat** at the top of the sidebar to start another.

Chats are grouped by when you last used them: Today, Yesterday, Previous 7
days, and Older. Type in **Search chats** to filter by title.

## Name, rename and delete

A new chat is titled from your first question. To change the title, touch and
hold a chat (right-click on a Mac), then choose **Rename**.

To delete a chat, swipe it to the left, or touch and hold and choose
**Delete**. **Delete** in the toolbar deletes the chat you have open and asks
first. Deleting removes its questions, answers and illustrations, and it
cannot be undone.

**Settings**, then **Delete all conversations**, removes every chat.

## Export a chat

**Export as Markdown** in the toolbar shares the chat you have open as a
Markdown file. Each question is a heading, with the scope, the engine and the
model that answered it, then the answer, then a **Sources** list of the five
best passages with their authors and scores. Send it to Notes, Files, Mail,
or anywhere else the share sheet reaches.

## Old chats stay as they were

A saved chat keeps each answer exactly as it was written. Changing the engine
or a setting does not rewrite old answers; use **Ask again** to see what the
new settings give.

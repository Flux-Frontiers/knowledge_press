# Browse and read the books

**Browse** is the library itself, for reading. It does not use a language
model or send anything anywhere.

Open it from the **Browse** tab on iPhone, or **Browse the corpus** in the
sidebar on iPad and Mac.

## Find a book

1. **Corpus** lists the genres, each with its number of books. Tap one to see
   its books, with their authors.
2. Tap a book to see its chapters. **Tree** beside a book opens the
   forest at its tree; see [The forest in the app](forest.md).
3. Tap a chapter to read it.

To find a book or an author, type in **Search titles and authors** at the
top of the Corpus list. Each word you type has to begin a word of the name
or title. Matching authors come first, each with their books below; then
**Titles** lists the other books whose title matches.

## Read a chapter

The chapter appears as text you can select and copy. The title and the
position (for example 3 / 24) are shown, with **Previous** and **Next**
buttons at the bottom. A very long chapter is split into parts, labelled
"Part 1 of 4" and so on.

## Diaries

The diaries open by date. A diary's chapters are grouped by year, and each
chapter is one dated entry. That includes Samuel Pepys's diary, John Evelyn's
two volumes, and Boswell's journal of his tour of the Hebrides.

## From an answer to the book

Under an answer, **Source passages** shows the passage that was found. To read
the whole book around it, find the book in Browse by its author.

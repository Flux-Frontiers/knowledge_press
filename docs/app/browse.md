# Browse and read the books

**Browse** is the library itself, for reading. It does not use a language
model or send anything anywhere.

Open it from the **Browse** tab on iPhone, or **Browse the corpus** in the
sidebar on iPad and Mac.

## Find a book

1. **Corpus** lists the genres, each with its number of books. Tap one to see
   its books, with their authors.
2. Tap a book to see its chapters. **Tree** beside a book opens the
   forest at its tree; see [The forest in the app](forest.md).
3. Tap a chapter to read it.

To find a book or an author, type in **Search titles and authors** at the
top of the Corpus list. Each word you type has to begin a word of the name
or title. Matching authors come first, each with their books below; then
**Titles** lists the other books whose title matches.

## Read a chapter

The chapter appears as text you can select and copy, set as a book: prose
fills the page as paragraphs, verse and plays keep their lines, italics show
as italics, and footnotes and illustration captions are small and gray. The
title and the position (for example 3 / 24) are shown, with **Previous** and
**Next** buttons at the bottom. A very long chapter is split into parts,
labelled "Part 1 of 4" and so on.

## Listen to a chapter

The play button in the toolbar reads the chapter aloud. The paragraph being
read is tinted and kept in view.

- The button again pauses, and the next press goes on from the same word.
- While a reading is going, tap a paragraph to read from there.
- At the end of a chapter the reading goes on to the next one.
- **Previous** or **Next**, or leaving the chapter, stops the reading.
- Footnotes and illustration captions are shown but not read.
- On iPhone and iPad the voice is heard with the ring switch on silent. It
  stops when the screen locks.

The voice matches the book's author: Jane Austen is read in a British
woman's voice, Mark Twain in an American man's. A translated author is read
in your device's accent, and a book with no known author in the system
voice. Premium and Enhanced voices are preferred when installed. To use one
voice for every book, and to set the speed, see **Reading aloud** in
[Settings](settings.md). Playing a chapter stops a book the forest is reading
aloud.

## Diaries

The diaries open by date. A diary's chapters are grouped by year, and each
chapter is one dated entry. That includes Samuel Pepys's diary, John Evelyn's
two volumes, and Boswell's journal of his tour of the Hebrides.

## From an answer to the book

Under an answer, **Source passages** shows the passage that was found. To read
the whole book around it, find the book in Browse by its author.

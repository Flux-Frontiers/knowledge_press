# Driving and the tour

The cart is not locked to the roads. Drive anywhere; the trunks and the
exhibits are solid, and everything else gives way.

## Drive

- Push forward to drive, back to reverse, sideways to steer.
- Release the throttle to stop. The cart coasts to a halt in about a second.
- Hold ++shift++ (right trigger on a gamepad) to hurry.
- ++space++ (left trigger) brakes harder than coasting.
- With the cart stopped, steering turns it in place.

**Driving pace** in settings switches between gentle exploration and brisk
driving.

## Look around

- ++left++ and ++right++, or the right stick, turn the view. It eases back
  to straight ahead when you release, so a glance to the side does not
  change where you are going.
- ++up++ and ++down++ tilt the view. The tilt stays where you leave it.
  Tilt up to see the crowns.

**Look sensitivity** in settings scales how far a push turns the view.

## Cameras

++c++ steps through four cameras. **Camera** in settings picks one directly.

| Camera | View |
| --- | --- |
| Behind the cart | The default. Follows a few meters behind, at head height. |
| High view | Behind and above, looking down at the cart and the road ahead. |
| In the cart | From the seat. The lantern is beside you. |
| God's eye | The whole forest from above the hub. |

## Take the guided tour

++q++, or the **Guided tour** button on touch, hands the cart to the tour.
The cart follows the roads by itself: out the nearest spoke, around the inner
ring, out along a spoke and around the outer ring, then back in to the
redwood. Lanterns
light the next 50 m of road ahead of it.

At each grove's stop the cart pulls up facing the signpost for 5 seconds and
shows a caption: how many books the grove holds, by whom, and its longest.
To hear the captions in the system voice, turn on **Read each grove aloud on
the ring** in settings. The first tour on a device opens with a short
welcome and a line on how the forest is made; later tours say a line.

The tour says when it has been around every grove, then drives back to the
redwood and ends there, facing it. The cart is yours again.

To hop off, steer, brake, or reverse. ++q++ or **End tour** also ends it.
The cart is yours again where you left it.

## Go home

++h++, or the home button in the top bar, returns you to the plaza in front
of the corpus redwood.

## Take a screenshot

The camera button in the top bar saves a picture of the forest without the
controls over it. On a phone it offers the picture to share; on a desktop it
downloads it.

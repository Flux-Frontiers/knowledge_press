# Settings

++esc++, or the settings button in the top bar, opens the settings. The cart
is parked while they are open. Every setting is saved in this browser.

## Driving and view

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Driving pace | **Gentle exploration** or **Brisk driving**: how fast the cart goes. |
| Look sensitivity | How far a push on the arrows or the stick turns the view. |
| Camera | Behind the cart, high view, in the cart, or god's eye. ++c++ steps through them. |
| Wind and gentle cart motion | The leaves move in the wind and the cart sways. Off by default when your system asks for reduced motion. |

## Detail and performance

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Leaf complexity | How many of each book's passages are drawn as leaves: **Low** one in ten, **Medium** one in four, **High** one in two, **Ultra** every one. Every level keeps the trees in proportion. Touch devices start at Low. Changing it regrows the forest. |
| Shadows and forest floor detail | Shadows, and the undergrowth along the roads. Turn off for a faster frame rate. |
| Show geometry and frame rate | A readout of triangles, draw calls, leaves and frames per second. |

## Sound and cards

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Read each grove aloud on the ring | On the guided tour, the captions are read in the system voice. Off by default. |
| Silent mode | No cards, hints or captions appear on their own. ++e++ and clicking a tree still work. |

## Environment

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Season | Spring, summer, autumn or winter. |
| Fog | Clear, light, normal or heavy standing haze. |
| Random weather | Morning fog that burns off, and cloud. Off by default. See [Fog and weather](sky.md#fog-and-weather). |

## Reset all settings

**Reset all settings**, at the bottom, asks first, then:

- Puts every setting above back to its default.
- Sets the season to summer and the clock to live.
- Empties the press.
- Forgets the tour's welcome, so the next tour gives it in full again.

The groves you have visited are kept.

# Reading and the press

## What appears as you approach

| You approach | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A tree | Its card appears once you slow down within about 7 m. |
| The corpus redwood | A card about the redwood appears within 16 m of the hub. Dismiss it and it stays away until you drive more than 20 m from the hub. |
| An exhibit's plaque or a grove's signpost | Nothing appears on its own. Tap the plaque to read it at reading size, or tap the signpost to list the grove's books. |
| A grove's stop on the guided tour | The tour shows a caption about the grove for 5 seconds. See [Driving and the tour](driving.md#take-the-guided-tour). |

Silent mode turns off the cards and captions that appear on their own. Tapping
always works.

## Read a tree

Drive up to a tree. Within about 7 m of the trunk, and with the cart slowed,
the book's **card** appears: its title, author, genre, an excerpt, and how
many leaves it has. The card has two buttons.

- **Press the book** (or ++e++, or A on a gamepad) puts the book in your
  press. Each pressed book rides in the cart's bed, in its grove's color.
- **Open the book** (or ++r++) opens the book's text.

On a phone or tablet the **Press** button beside the stick reads the nearest
tree into the press. Next to a tree already in your press it says **Read**
and opens the book.

Clicking a tree pins its card, so you can read the card of a tree you are not
next to. Clicking open ground clears it.

**Silent mode** stops cards from appearing on their own. ++e++ still reads
the nearest tree, ++r++ still opens it, and clicking a tree still shows its
card. Turn it on from
settings.

## Open the book

**Open the book**, or ++r++, shows the book's text in the reader, a chapter
at a time. ++r++ opens the tree whose card is showing, or else the nearest
tree within reach.

- Pick a chapter from the menu at the top, or use **Previous** and **Next**
  at the bottom.
- ++left++ and ++right++ turn the chapter. ++up++ and ++down++ scroll the
  page.
- The two **A** buttons beside the close button make the text smaller or
  larger, in four steps. The size is saved for later visits.
- ++esc++, the close button, or a tap outside the page closes the reader. It
  remembers the chapter you were at for the rest of the visit.

## Listen to the book

The play button at the top of the reader, or ++space++, reads the chapter
aloud. The paragraph being read is tinted and kept in view.

- ++space++ or the button again pauses, and the next press goes on from the
  same word. After the screen has locked, it starts the paragraph again.
- While a reading is going, tap a paragraph to read from there.
- At the end of a chapter the reading goes on to the next one, and it stops
  at the end of the book.
- Closing the reader, or turning to another chapter yourself, stops the
  reading. The book remembers the paragraph, so the play button starts there
  next time, on this visit or a later one.
- Footnotes and illustration captions are shown but not read.

The voice matches the book's author: Jane Austen is read in a British
woman's voice, Mark Twain in a man's. A translated author is read in your
device's accent; a book with many authors in the system voice. Voices on
your device come before ones a browser fetches over the network. Settings has
a **Reading voice** to pick one voice for every book, and a **Reading
speed**. See [Settings](settings.md).

On an iPhone or iPad the reading stops when the screen locks. When you come
back, the paragraph it stopped in is read again from its start. On a Mac it
reads on with the screen locked.

Prose is set as paragraphs that fill the page, whatever the printed
edition's line length was. Verse and plays keep their lines; a line too long
for the page continues indented below. Words the edition marked in italics
are set in italics. Footnote marks are small raised numbers, footnotes and
sidenotes are set small and gray, and illustrations are shown as their
captions.

Chapters are the book's own sections. A very long section is split into
parts, shown as "(Part 1 of 4)" and so on. A diary's chapters are its dated
entries.

The text comes from a file per book served next to the page. On the
published site every book is there. In a local checkout the files have to be
generated first; see [Book text](../BOOK_TEXT.md).

## The press

The press is the list of books you have read. ++l++, or the **Press** tab under
**Browse** in the top bar, opens it. The Browse button shows how many books
the press holds. Each entry opens the book or jumps to its tree.

A book stays in the press until you empty it: **Empty the press**, at the
bottom of the Press tab, asks first, then takes every book out. The quests
that count books start over. Reset all settings leaves the press alone. The count is saved in this
browser; see [Where your progress lives](getting-started.md#where-your-progress-lives).

## Quests

Five hints appear as you drive, each a small thing to do in the forest:

| Quest | Done when |
| --- | --- |
| First impressions | You have read three trees. |
| A Stoic | Your press holds *Meditations*, Epictetus, or the *Enchiridion*. |
| The Dane | Your press holds *Hamlet*. |
| Bare wood | You have switched the season to winter. |
| Across the press | You have visited four genre groves. |

A hint can be dismissed. Silent mode hides them. They are for your own
amusement; nothing is locked behind them.
